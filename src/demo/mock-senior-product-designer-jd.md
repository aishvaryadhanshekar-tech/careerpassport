# Senior Product Designer

**Location:** Bengaluru · Hybrid (3 days a week in our Indiranagar office)
**Experience:** 6–9 years
**Compensation:** ₹38–55L per year fixed, plus ESOPs
**Type:** Full-time · B2B SaaS product company · Design team

## About us

We're a Series C B2B SaaS product company building finance-operations software — invoicing, approvals and spend controls — used by more than 2,000 businesses across India and Southeast Asia. The design team is 14 people across four product pods, with a shared design system, an embedded research practice and weekly cross-pod critique.

## About the role

This is a new position. As a Senior Product Designer you will own end-to-end design for our invoicing and approvals surface — from problem framing and research through shipped release — and help evolve the design system alongside two other senior designers. You'll sit in a pod with a product manager, an engineering manager and six engineers, and report to the Head of Design.

This is a senior individual contributor role: you'll set the craft bar for your surface, bring clarity to ambiguous workflow problems and help mid-level designers grow.

## What you'll do

- Own discovery through delivery for invoicing and approvals, from problem framing to measured release.
- Turn messy finance-team workflows — multi-level approvals, permissions, bulk actions — into clear, shippable designs.
- Plan and run usability tests and customer interviews, and use Mixpanel data to validate outcomes.
- Design every state that matters in enterprise software: empty, error, loading, permission and data-dense views.
- Prototype at the right fidelity in Figma to answer the open question quickly.
- Contribute components, patterns and tokens back to the design system and help drive adoption.
- Partner with PM and Engineering on scope and trade-offs, hand off cleanly and QA what ships.
- Run and take part in critique, giving sharp, specific feedback.

## What we're looking for

- 6+ years of product design, with at least 3 years designing complex B2B / SaaS workflows (approvals, permissions, data-dense tables, multi-step setup)
- A portfolio with 2+ end-to-end shipped case studies that show problem framing, their own decisions and trade-offs, and measurable outcomes
- Systems thinking: has built or meaningfully evolved a design system — components, tokens, documentation and adoption
- Strong interaction and visual craft in Figma, including auto layout, variants, variables and high-fidelity interactive prototypes
- Research-informed decisions: plans and runs usability tests and interviews, and uses product analytics to validate outcomes
- Partners closely with PM and Engineering on scope and trade-offs, with clean, annotated dev handoff and QA of what ships
- Clear written and verbal communication — frames design rationale crisply and gives and receives critique well

## Nice to have

- Experience designing for finance, payments or accounting teams.
- Accessibility practice built into your process (WCAG 2.1 AA).
- Front-end literacy — enough to prototype in code or hold a real conversation about feasibility.
- Fluency with AI tools in your design workflow.
- Experience mentoring designers.

## Interview process

- Portfolio screen — share two case studies with a short note on each (about 20 minutes).
- Recruiter conversation (30 minutes).
- Design exercise — take-home with a 3-hour timebox, or a 90-minute live session if you prefer.
- Portfolio presentation and craft review with the Head of Design and two senior designers (60 minutes).
- Cross-functional round with a product manager and a staff engineer (45 minutes).
- Hiring manager conversation with the Head of Design (45 minutes).
